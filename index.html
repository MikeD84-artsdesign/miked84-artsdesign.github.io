My Story

"Hello! I am Michael de Leon, a dedicated Virtual Assistant service provider and Freelancer specializing in comprehensive business support and creative digital solutions.

My professional journey is rooted in years of high-pressure experience within the business process industry a.k.a call center. Worked as account specialist in an internet provider industry based in US where I excelled in technical and sales support, handling high-volume calls everyday and hitting monthly sales quota. Transitioning from Technical and Sales to retail customer service and to the freelance space, I have mastered the art of managing complex tasks while maintaining a professional driven mindset.
